# Third-party notices

The PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 covers dailyo's original software. The dailyo and game names and the logo artwork in `dist/assets/` are excluded from that grant. The materials below have their own sources and terms.

- **Flag artwork.** The images in `dist/games/flags/` come from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) under the MIT License. The bundled [copyright and license notice](https://trainrot.vercel.app/games/flags/LICENSE) is in `dist/games/flags/LICENSE`.
- **Language excerpts.** `dist/games/data/languages.json` contains excerpts from the [Universal Declaration of Human Rights translations](https://www.ohchr.org/en/human-rights/universal-declaration/translations). The excerpts were drawn from the `udhr2` corpus, which [NLTK lists as public domain](https://github.com/nltk/nltk_data/blob/gh-pages/DATASET-LICENSES.md#public-domain).
- **World map and city coordinates.** The outline and city points in `dist/games/data/maprot.json` were derived from [Natural Earth’s land polygons](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-physical-vectors/110m-land/) and [populated places](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/110m-populated-places/), which are [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
- **Borderrot map outlines.** `dist/games/data/border-map.json` uses [Natural Earth 50m admin-0 map units, v5.1.2](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_map_units.geojson), which are public domain.
- **Country neighbours.** The border graph in `dist/games/data/borderrot.json` adapts [GeoNames country information](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/countryInfo.txt) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). We keep reciprocal land borders, omit overseas territories and the Guantánamo lease, treat Kosovo as a separate node from Serbia, and adjust a few display names.

- **Ladder words.** `dist/games/data/ladder-words.json` is the four-letter subset of the public-domain ENABLE word list, compiled by Alan Beale and maintained by Mendel Cooper. [Source list](https://github.com/dolph/dictionary/blob/master/enable1.txt) · [ENABLE readme](https://github.com/BartMassey/wordlists/blob/main/README-enable2k.txt)

## Subway data

The Harbour City dataset is fictional and generated by `scripts/build-demo-data.mjs`. The deployed NYC edition uses separately supplied MTA transit data and NYC Planning borough boundaries. The repository includes tools for preparing those inputs. If you use those tools with NYC data, review the [MTA data feed terms](https://www.mta.info/developers/terms-and-conditions) and [NYC Open Data terms](https://cityofnewyork.github.io/opendatatsm/publicpolicies.html).

## New visual games and price snapshots

Skyrot, Skylinerot, and Zoomrot bundle photographs with per-file source, author, and license in `dist/games/data/{skyrot,skylinerot,zoomrot}.json`. Their answer reveals link to the original source. Crop and resize transformations are used for gameplay.

Pricerot uses factual price snapshots from Trader Joe’s public product catalog, recorded 2026-11-17, with exact product source URLs in `dist/games/data/pricerot.json`. Product illustrations are original dailyo SVG artwork and do not reproduce retailer logos or packaging. Prices are before tax and are not live offers. Trader Joe’s does not sponsor this game.

Orderrot uses NASA planet comparison data, NIST measurement definitions, and mathematical facts. Each question links to its source.
