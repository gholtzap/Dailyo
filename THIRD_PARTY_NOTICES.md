## Subway data

The Harbour City dataset is fictional and generated by `scripts/build-demo-data.mjs`. The deployed NYC edition uses separately supplied MTA transit data and NYC Planning borough boundaries. The repository includes tools for preparing those inputs. If you use those tools with NYC data, review the [MTA data feed terms](https://www.mta.info/developers/terms-and-conditions) and [NYC Open Data terms](https://cityofnewyork.github.io/opendatatsm/publicpolicies.html).

## New visual games and price snapshots

Skyrot, Skylinerot, and Zoomrot bundle photographs with per-file source, author, and license in `dist/games/data/{skyrot,skylinerot,zoomrot}.json`. Their answer reveals link to the original source. Crop and resize transformations are used for gameplay. Each deck contains 36 distinct subjects. The curated source files and crop settings are recorded in `scripts/visual-photo-sources.json`; `scripts/fetch-visual-assets.py` rebuilds the bundled decks while retaining each photograph’s attribution and license.

Pricerot uses factual price snapshots from Trader Joe’s public product catalog, recorded 2026-11-17, with exact product source URLs in `dist/games/data/pricerot.json`. Product photographs are © Trader Joe’s and are bundled locally for identification of the exact products. Each item records its photograph URL in `imageSource`. The Assorted Belgian Chocolates photo is credited to Trader Joe’s by Martha Stewart; its source page is recorded in `imageSourcePage`. These photos and product packaging are outside dailyo’s code license. Prices are before tax and are not live offers. Trader Joe’s does not sponsor this game.

Orderrot uses NASA planet comparison data, NIST measurement definitions, and mathematical facts. Each question links to its source.
